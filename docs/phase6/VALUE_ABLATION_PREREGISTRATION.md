# OI incremental-value preregistration mechanism disposition

Status: VALUE_ABLATION_PREREGISTRATION_BLOCKED_MECHANISM. Date:2026-10-27.
This records the narrow blocker allowed by the consolidated Phase6A1 engineering brief.
It does not block OI source/feature-foundation engineering, authorize a study or enable OI.

## Outcome-blind review basis

Only frozen pre-result definitions in [Phase3 DATA_PLAN](../phase3/DATA_PLAN.md) and
[research methodology](../RESEARCH_AND_VALIDATION.md) were inspected for this decision.
No consumed OOS dataset, result, metric or outcome was opened to select a baseline.
The pre-result comparator set includes NO_TRADE, PASSIVE and fixed-rule24h trend;
none is called best, and none is selected using Phase3 performance.

## Exact missing mechanism

The accepted Phase6A1 mechanisms define provider-native OI level, adjacent hourly change,
and within-symbol fractional change, plus an OI-only extrapolation/persistence forecast.
They define no causal mapping from these OI facts to price-candidate admission or rejection.
A positive or negative OI change does not identify long-versus-short positioning or a price
forecast under the admitted snapshot schema. An OI sign, size, forecast or clipping threshold
used to accept/reject a price candidate would add an unaccepted hypothesis and arbitrary
rule; no threshold or direction follows from the accepted OI-FWD mechanism.

The frozen price-only comparators supply an outcome-blind comparator set, but they do
not supply the missing OI augmentation. Choosing one comparator now cannot repair that
mechanism gap. Accordingly this run does not fabricate a completed value preregistration,
select a comparator by consumed outcomes or invent empirical success thresholds.

## Future prerequisite, not an authorized study

Before value claims, OI enablement or progression on OI-value grounds, independently review
an explicit outcome-blind OI-to-candidate mechanism and baseline-selection rule under
then-current source rights and accepted dependencies. Separately preregister a paired
with/without-OI replay on the same future/unconsumed evidence, changing only the OI rule
and preserving market inputs, candidate logic, costs, risk, timing and replay semantics.
That later record must account for coverage, saved losses, foregone gains, uncertainty,
cost/latency where applicable, dependence, multiplicity and no retune. There is no grid,
threshold search, future-data fit or implicit OOS reuse authority here.

Both consumed Phase3 OOS holdouts remain permanently unavailable for selection/reuse.
Engineering fixture results establish no PnL, price alpha, live authority or incremental
value. OI-FWD-v1 remains predictability-only; the canonical Phase6 value gate remains OPEN.
