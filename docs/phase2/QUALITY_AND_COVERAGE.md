# Phase 2 history coverage and expansion

There is no fixed five-year or ten-year research horizon. The five-year warm-store
default remains a planning policy, separately scoped by [ADR-012](../adr/012-phase2-data-boundaries.md).
Accepted pilot hourly artifacts are preserved under byte quotas and rights review,
without age-triggered deletion. Calendar length does not establish sufficient samples.

The operator pilot selects BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT spot, hourly klines:

| Selection | Purpose | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2024-12-31 and 2025-01-01 | Real-provider unit-transition/parser integrity | Two days do not characterize a regime |
| March 2020 | Selected historical volatility/disruption stress window | Not current execution conditions |
| November 2022 | Selected crypto market disruption window | Venue/product and structural changes matter |
| June 2024 | Additional calendar/regime contrast | No statistical regime label or sufficiency claim |
| September 2026 | Recent completed calendar month | Historical receipt remains unknown |

Actual earliest/latest, rows, findings, source checksum/version, availability confidence,
rights and retained bytes are generated by `auvexo-data coverage` from accepted manifests.
Requested periods are not coverage claims. See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) for the
actual machine-readable matrix and failed/blocked provider attempts, if any.

The actual [coverage matrix](evidence/coverage.json) contains twelve historical partitions
and four live partitions. Each asset has 2,950 retained hourly rows: 24 on each boundary
day, 742/744 in March 2020, and 720/720 in each selected later month. Both March archives
have a short 09:00 UTC candle on March 4 ending at 09:21:46.694 (BTC) / .700 (ETH), and
no 10:00 row. The full-interval boundary check rejects the short candle (reported under
TIMESTAMP_UNIT), leaving two explicit gaps per asset; these partitions are DEGRADED.
No explanation of the provider interruption is inferred. Original ZIP bytes are retained.

Live proof on 2026-10-17 covers closed 1m intervals [05:24,05:27) UTC for both assets,
three rows each, all PASS. Actual receipts span 05:26:34.311819 through 05:28:03.196993
UTC. This is a small public pilot, not seven-day qualification. The retained 16 partitions
contain 5,906 rows, 338,811 raw bytes and 625,427 Parquet bytes. Parquet is larger than
the already compressed source ZIPs because it retains full provenance and tiny batches.

Earliest historical coverage is March 2020; latest selected month ends 2026-10-01 UTC.
All unselected periods, including intervening years and pre-2020 availability, remain
UNKNOWN. These endpoints do not mean continuous six-year coverage or earliest listing.
No fixed five-/ten-year cap or claim of research sample sufficiency is imposed.

BTC/ETH Exness CFDs and XAU Exness CFD remain distinct rows with unknown symbols/specs,
zero observed rows and EXTERNALLY BLOCKED source status. Spot USDT prices never fill
those holes or stand in for CFD bid/ask, spread, financing, margin, lot or session facts.

Staged expansion: first pass the integrity pilot; then request selected older/recent
months only for a preregistered future experiment or missing stress condition. Prefer
hourly resolution for cheap historical diversity; use selective 1m daily windows for
questions needing that resolution. Do not retain redundant resolutions automatically.
Use actual listing/provider availability, missing years, structural breaks, rights,
compressed bytes and out-of-sample needs to choose further coverage. No pre-inception
or unavailable data may be reconstructed, filled with current facts or invented.

Quality status is per dataset/range. Duplicate identical rows collapse with a finding;
conflicting duplicates block the batch. Missing bars remain gaps, malformed rows are
excluded with typed findings, and partial live candles cannot become finalized data.
Corrections preserve lineage and impact counts. `require_complete` and strict as-of
reads fail closed on incomplete/unavailable data. Historical backtest feasibility and
profitability are Phase 3 questions and are not established by Phase 2 ingestion.
