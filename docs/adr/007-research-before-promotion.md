# ADR-007: research before promotion

Status: FROZEN
Date: 2026-10-15
Requirements: R-005, R-006, R-007, R-008

## Context

Backtest selection bias and feature complexity can masquerade as edge.

## Decision

Preregister hypotheses/splits/costs, preserve all trials, use holdout/walk-forward and simple baselines. Mandatory rejected-trade book and paired incremental-value analysis. Champion releases require evidence and owner gate.

## Alternatives

Win-rate optimization, agent confidence, post-hoc holdout tuning and autonomous live promotion rejected.

## Consequences

NO EDGE and NO TRADE are valid. Operational qualification does not prove alpha. Suspend degradation without uncontrolled self-modification.

## Validation

Phase 3 leakage/reference/replay; Phase 6 ablations; Phase 8 eligible champion and independent review.

## Review point

Research and validation; OD-15 thresholds frozen per study before holdout observation.
